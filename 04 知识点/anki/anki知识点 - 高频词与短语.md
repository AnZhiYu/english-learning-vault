# Anki 知识点：高频词与短语

> [[anki知识点]] 的词汇与短语整理版。优先记“搭配 + 完整例句”，不要只背中文释义。

## 花费：`cost` / `spend` / `pay` / `take`

| 词 | 常用结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cost` | `sth. costs sb. money` | The ticket cost me 50 yuan. |
| `spend` | `sb. spends time/money on sth. / (in) doing` | I spend an hour reading every day. |
| `pay` | `pay money for sth.` / `pay for sth.` | I paid for the meal. |
| `take` | `It takes sb. time to do` | It takes me 30 minutes to get to work. |

相关名词：`fee`（服务/入场费）、`fare`（交通票价）、`charge`（收费金额）、`cost`（成本）。

## 基础动作词

### `cut` / `hurt` / `hit` / `let`

- `cut`：cut the cake into eight pieces; cut down on sugar; cut off the electricity; cut in line.
- `hurt`：身体或感情受伤。My back hurts. / His words hurt me. / I feel hurt.
- `hit`：击中；达到；突然想到。hit the ball; hit one million page views; It hit me that ...
- `let`：`let sb. do`，不加 `to`。Let me help. / Don't let me down. / Let's go and listen to Zhao Lei's music.

## 高频动词：`go` 与 `get`

### `go`

| 短语 | 核心意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `go on doing` | 继续同一件事 | She went on talking. |
| `go on to do` | 接着做另一件事 | He went on to study abroad. |
| `go ahead` | 请便；开始 | Can I start? - Go ahead. |
| `go back to ...` / `go back home` | 回到某地 / 回家 | Let's go back to the hotel. / I went back home. |
| `go out` | 出门；灯熄灭 | The lights went out. |
| `go with` | 与……搭配；随同 | This shirt goes well with jeans. |
| `go for` | 选择；争取 | I'll go for the salad. |
| `go off` | 闹钟响；爆炸；变质 | My alarm went off at 7. / The milk has gone off. |
| `go over` | 复习；检查；过去 | Let's go over the answers. |
| `go after` | 追逐；争取 | She is going after her dream. |
| `go by` | 流逝；经过；按照 | As time goes by, ... / Go by the rules. |

`go out with` 可表示“和某人外出”，在恋爱语境中也可指约会；要靠上下文判断。

### `get`

`get` 的核心是“获得、到达、变成、理解或进入一种状态”。过去式 `got`；过去分词英式常用 `got`，美式常见 `gotten`。

| 短语 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| get a job / ticket / degree / call / text | I got a text from my friend. |
| get married | They got married last year. |
| get on / off | She got on the bus at 8. |
| get in / out of | Get in the car. / Get out of the room. |
| get ahead | He works hard to get ahead in his career. |
| get by | We don't have much money, but we get by. |
| get over | It took her weeks to get over the flu. |
| get rid of | I want to get rid of this bad habit. |
| get along with | I get along well with my coworkers. |

## `make`、`take`、`think`

### `make`

- 制作/造成：make a cake; make a mistake.
- 使得：make me happy; make it clear.
- 固定搭配：make sure; make progress; make sense; make money; make a decision.
- `make up`：编造、化妆、和好。He made up an excuse.
- `make it`：成功做到、准时到达。I hope you can make it to the meeting.

> `make progress` 是“取得进步”；`get ahead` 是“在事业或竞争中领先”，两者不完全等同。

### `take`

- `take a break / walk / nap / shower / picture / seat / exam`。
- `take care of`：照顾/处理。I'll take care of the plants.
- `take place`：发生。The meeting will take place tomorrow. 不说 `was taken place`。
- `take advantage of`：利用机会，也可能指“占某人便宜”，要看宾语。
- `take over`：接管。The new CEO took over the company.
- `take part in`：参加活动。She took part in the marathon.
- `take a rain check`：这次先不参加，希望下次再约（常用于美式口语）。

### `think`

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `think + 从句` | 认为 | I think it will rain. |
| `think about` | 仔细考虑 | Let me think about your suggestion. |
| `think of` | 想起；考虑某个想法 | I'm thinking of changing my job. |
| `think over` | 认真考虑 | Think it over before you decide. |

- 观点意义通常不用进行时：✅ I think you are right. ❌ I'm thinking you are right.
- 疑问句：What do you think will happen?（不是 `Do you think what ...`）
- 更自然的否定转移：I don't think he is right.

## `look`、`put`、`turn`

### `look`

| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| look at | 看；考虑 | Look at this photo. |
| look for | 寻找 | I'm looking for my keys. |
| look after | 照顾 | She looks after her grandmother. |
| look forward to | 期待（后接名词/doing） | I look forward to meeting you. |
| look into | 调查 | We need to look into the problem. |
| look up | 查阅；抬头看；好转 | Look the word up in a dictionary. |
| look like | 看起来像 | He looks like his father. |
| look as if / though | 看起来好像 | It looks as if it is going to rain. |
| look around | 环顾；随便看看 | Feel free to look around. |
| look down on | 看不起 | Don't look down on others. |

### `put` 与 `turn`

- `put on` 穿上；`put off` 推迟；`put out` 熄灭；`put up with` 忍受；`put down` 放下；`put together` 组装；`put ... into ...` 投入/放入。
- `turn on / off` 打开/关闭；`turn up / down` 调高/调低；`turn into` 变成；`turn out (to be)` 结果是；`turn around` 转身；`turn over` 翻面/交出。
- 可分短语中的代词放中间：turn **it** off; put **it** on; check **it** off.

## `check`：核实、登记与检查

| 表达 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| check | 检查、核实 | Check your answers. |
| check in | 办理入住/登记；报到 | We checked in at the hotel. |
| check out | 退房；借出；仔细看看 | We must check out by 11. |
| check off | 勾掉/打勾 | Check off each completed task. |
| check into | 入住；调查 | The police are checking into the case. |
| check with sb. | 向某人核实/征求许可 | Please check with your manager first. |
| check on / check up on | 查看情况、关心或检查 | The doctor checked on the patient. |
| check over | 粗略检查 | Check over the contract. |

`check through` 可以表示“从头检查”，但日常更常用 `go through`：Let's go through the document.

## 易混词与常见搭配

| 对比 | 正确用法 |
| --- | --- |
| borrow / lend / keep | borrow sth. from sb.; lend sth. to sb.; keep sth. for two weeks. |
| bring / take | bring 到说话者/目的地一侧；take 带离说话者。Bring the book here; take it there. |
| beat / win | beat a person/team/opponent; win a game/prize. |
| advice / advise | advice 不可数；advise sb. to do / advise doing. |
| say / tell | say something / say that ...; tell sb. something / tell sb. to do. |
| alive / living | alive 常作表语或后置定语：the only man alive；living 常作前置定语：a living writer。 |
| alone / lonely | alone = 独自；lonely = 感到孤独。 |
| bored / boring | 人感到 bored；事物令人 boring。 |
| accept / receive | receive 强调收到；accept 强调接受。 |
| among / between | between 常用于两者，也可用于明确列出的多者；among 用于不明确的多者之中。 |
| another / other / others / the other(s) | another + 单数；other + 复数/不可数名词；others 不接名词；the other 指两者中的另一个。 |

## 词汇复习规则

```text
不记：go = 去
改记：go over = 复习/检查
例句：Let's go over the answers before class.
易错：go over the answers，不是 go over to the answers。
```

遇到新短语时，优先补进对应动词标题；不确定是否正确时，先写 `待核对`，不要把猜测直接做成 Anki 正面答案。
