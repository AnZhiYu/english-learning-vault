## 四、训练设计

- 故事：一次国内旅行、徒步或最想实现的深度旅行；
- 句型：I went to...／What impressed me most was...／I'd like to... because...；
- 听力重点：抓地点、时间、评价、原因，不追每个词；
- 修复表达：Did you say...?／I caught the place, but I missed the reason.
- 意外追问：Would you rather travel alone or with your family?
