## `there be` 与 `it`

### `there be`：表示“某处/某时有……”

| 时态 | 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 现在 | `There is / are ...` | There are two books on the desk. |
| 过去 | `There was / were ...` | There was a meeting yesterday. |
| 现在完成 | `There has / have been ...` | There have been many changes. |
| 将来 | `There will be ...` | There will be a test next week. |

- 否定：`There isn't / aren't ...`；`There wasn't / weren't ...`。
- 谓语通常跟随真正主语的单复数：`There is a book and two pens ...`。

### `it`：指代或形式主语

- 指前文事物：I bought a book. **It** is useful.
- 指时间、天气、距离、环境：It's late. / It's raining. / How far is it from home to work?
- 形式主语：It's nice **to meet you**. / It's important **to practise every day**.

> `there` 说“存在”，`it` 说“某个已知事物”或承担形式主语位置。

## 时间、地点与方向介词

### 地点：`in` / `on` / `at`

| 介词 | 核心感觉 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `in` | 在内部、较大的范围 | in the room; in Hangzhou; in a car; in a taxi |
| `on` | 在表面、公共交通工具上 | on the table; on the bus; on the second floor |
| `at` | 在点位、活动或机构 | at the bus stop; at a party; at the doctor's |

### 时间：`in` / `on` / `at`

- `in` + 月、季节、年份、上午/下午：in October; in 2026; in the morning.
- `on` + 星期、日期、特定某天的早中晚：on Friday; on May 1; on a cold morning.
- `at` + 时刻、夜晚、节日的某一点：at 7 o'clock; at night.
- `this / that / last / next` 前通常不加时间介词：this afternoon; last Sunday.

### `home` 与方向

- `go / come / get / arrive / return home`：`home` 前不加 `to`。
- `go to the park`、`arrive at the airport`、`arrive in Hangzhou`。
- `get to` 后接地点；`reach` 直接接地点：get to the station / reach the station.

## `to do`、`doing` 与使役结构

### 常见搭配

| 结构 | 常见动词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `verb + to do` | want, plan, hope, decide, expect, offer, promise, learn, refuse | I plan to practise speaking every day. |
| `verb + doing` | enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, suggest, practise | She enjoys swimming. |
| 两者都可，意思相近或略有差别 | like, love, hate, prefer, begin, start, continue | I like reading before bed. |
| `stop doing` / `stop to do` | 停止正在做的事 / 停下来去做另一件事 | He stopped smoking. / He stopped to smoke. |

### “让某人做”

| 结构 | 例句 | 注意 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ask / tell / advise / expect / persuade / teach sb. to do` | The teacher told us to listen carefully. | 这些动词后用 `to do`。 |
| `make / let sb. do` | The joke made me laugh. / Let me try. | **不加** `to`。 |
| `help sb. (to) do` | She helped me (to) fix the bug. | `to` 可省。 |

## `need`、能力与习惯

### `need`

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `need to do` | 需要做 | I need to finish this report. |
| `don't / doesn't need to do` | 不需要做 | You don't need to hurry. |
| `needn't do` | 不必做（现在/将来；英式更常见） | You needn't bring anything. |
| `needn't have done` | 做了，但其实没必要做 | You needn't have bought so much food. |
| `sth. needs doing` | 某事需要被做 | The room needs cleaning. |

### `can` 与 `be able to`

- `can`：一般能力或许可。I can swim.
- `be able to`：可用于更多时态。She has been able to solve the problem.
- 过去某次成功完成，常用 `was / were able to`：We were able to catch the last bus.

### `used to`

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `used to do` | 过去常常做，现在不再如此 | I used to live in Ganzhou. |
| `be used to doing / noun` | 习惯于 | I am used to getting up early. |
| `be used to do` | 被用来做 | This room is used to hold meetings. |

## 情态动词与条件句

### 情态动词 + 完成式：对过去的推测

- `must have done`：一定做过。She must have left already.
- `may / might have done`：可能做过。He may have missed the bus.
- `can't have done`：不可能做过。They can't have finished so quickly.

### 条件句

| 类型 | 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 真实可能 | `If + 一般现在时, will + do` | If it rains, we will stay home. |
| 与现在相反 | `If + 过去式 / were, would/could/might + do` | If I were you, I would ask first. |
| 与过去相反 | `If + had done, would/could/might have done` | If he had left earlier, he might have caught the train. |

## 分词作状语

- **主语必须一致**：分词短语的逻辑主语应与主句主语相同。
- 主动、同时发生：`doing`。Walking along the river, **I** saw a bird.
- 被动：`done`。Surprised by the news, **she** said nothing.
- 先发生：`having done`。Having finished my work, I went home.

不要写成主语不一致的“悬垂分词”：

- ❌ Walking along the river, a bird was seen.
- ✅ Walking along the river, I saw a bird.

## 连词与并列结构

### 基础连词

- `and` 补充；`but` 转折；`or` 选择；`so` 结果；`because` 原因。
- `although / though` 不与 `but` 同句并用：
  - ✅ Although it was raining, we went out.
  - ❌ Although it was raining, but we went out.
- `because` 不与 `so` 同句并用。
- `as soon as` 引导时间状语从句，谈将来时从句用一般现在时：I'll call you as soon as I arrive.

### `both` / `either` / `neither`

| 结构 | 意思 | 谓语 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `both A and B` | 两者都 | 复数：Both Tom and Lily are ready. |
| `either A or B` | A 或 B | 就近：Either you or he is right. |
| `neither A nor B` | A 和 B 都不 | 就近：Neither you nor he is ready. |
| `not only A but also B` | 不但 A 而且 B | 连接主语时也就近 |

## 宾语从句与定语从句

### 宾语从句：一个句子作宾语

| 原句类型 | 引导词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 陈述句 | `(that)` | I think (that) he is right. |
| 一般疑问句 | `if / whether` | I don't know whether he will come. |
| 特殊疑问句 | 原疑问词 | Do you know where she lives? |

**三条规则**：使用陈述语序；注意时态；`say` 不直接接“人 + 从句”。

- ❌ I don't know what does he want.
- ✅ I don't know what he wants.
- ❌ She said me that she was tired.
- ✅ She told me that she was tired. / She said that she was tired.

### 定语从句：修饰前面的名词

- 人：`who / whom / that`；物：`which / that`；所属关系：`whose`。
- 时间、地点、原因：`when / where / why`。
- 关系词在从句中作成分；宾语位置的关系代词有时可以省略。

```text
I know the girl who is standing there.
This is the village where he was born.
```

在“人和物并列、最高级、序数词、the only、all/everything/nothing”等传统考试语境中，通常优先用 `that`。
