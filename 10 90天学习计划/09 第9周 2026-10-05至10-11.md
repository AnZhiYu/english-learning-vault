## 四、训练设计

- 故事结构：Before...／After...／I realized...／Now I'm trying to...；
- 句型重点：观点＋原因＋个人例子；
- 听力重点：人物故事中的转折和情绪；
- 修复重点：What I'm trying to say is...／A simple example is...；
- 意外追问：Do you think having a family limits freedom or changes its meaning?
